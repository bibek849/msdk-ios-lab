# Community conduct

We welcome people of different backgrounds, experience levels and tool preferences. Hardware ownership, paid AI access and prior DJI experience are not prerequisites for contributing.

- Treat people respectfully. Critique code, evidence and decisions rather than personal traits.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing or unwanted contact.
- Give credit, disclose uncertainty and correct inaccurate claims. Do not fabricate test results or present someone else's work as your own.
- Keep discussion relevant. Avoid mass-generated issues, repeated unsolicited PRs and pressure for hardware access or immediate reviews.
- Respect privacy and project boundaries, including requests to stop an interaction.

The maintainer, [@bibek849](https://github.com/bibek849), may ask for changes, remove inappropriate material, lock a discussion or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and explain the relevant behavior without exposing private details. A respectful appeal can ask for reconsideration with new context.

For a non-sensitive project concern, open a concise issue without unnecessary personal information. For harassment, threats or exposed private information, use GitHub's report-abuse controls rather than reposting the material here. A dedicated private maintainer conduct channel has not yet been published; do not assume public issues are confidential.

These original guidelines apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions. They do not appoint an agent as a moderator or authorize contacting people outside the project.
